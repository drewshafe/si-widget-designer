When a customer reports a problem

If the order has a ShipInsure line, send them to ShipInsure to file a claim. No ShipInsure line means no coverage.

Add it to a draft order
4 steps
  1. 1
    Build the draft with the customer's items.
  2. 2
    Search products for “ShipInsure.”
  3. 3
    Pick the variant whose SKU matches the merchandise total.
    SI_LEVEL_0-100
    $0–$100 orders
    That variant's price is the protection fee.
  4. 4
    Add one unit, then convert the draft to an order.
    Coverage starts the moment it's an order.
Before you convert
Drafts aren't protected
